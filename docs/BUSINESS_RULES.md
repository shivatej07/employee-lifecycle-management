# Business Rules
BR-001: A task cannot be completed while prerequisites are incomplete.
BR-002: Access cannot be provisioned before effective start date.
BR-003: Manager approval does not itself provision access; IT provisions after eligibility checks.
BR-004: A physical asset may have at most one active assignment.
BR-005: Terminated employees must not retain active grants.
BR-006: Offboarding cannot complete while mandatory revocation/recovery work is open.
BR-007: Critical state transitions must be audited.
BR-008: Overrides require authorization and a reason.
